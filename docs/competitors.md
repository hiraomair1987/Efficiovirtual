## What to learn from them
1. Pages for each service in each place (e.g. "Google Ads management UK") — each with genuinely different content.
2. A clear, memorable framework (ours: Understand, Create, Improve).
3. Industry pages (e.g. SaaS companies).
4. Transparent starting prices — only if they are real efficiovirtual prices.
5. Proof: case studies, reviews, client logos (only with permission).

## What not to copy
- Their results figures or "best agency" claims (self-reported, unverified).
- Offices or local teams efficiovirtual doesn't have.
- Their exact wording.
