# Blog keyword plan (from competitor informational keywords)

Built on 2026-11-05 from the owner's Semrush exports:
SmartSites (USA, 1,000 keywords), Outlier Digital (Canada, 226), Adnika (UAE, 17 + 54), MetaTech (UAE, 60).

**How the list was chosen**
- Kept only informational keywords ("I" intent). Removed brand names, city service keywords
  (e.g. "seo mississauga" — those belong on service/location pages, not blog posts)
  and topics unrelated to efficiovirtual's services (virtual receptionists, order tracking, Magento, direct mail gifting…).
- Ranked by: fit with our services and case studies → difficulty (KD) → search volume.
  A new website should start with **KD ≤ 25**; harder topics come later.
- Volume = monthly searches in that competitor's Semrush country database (mostly USA; UAE for Adnika/MetaTech; Canada for Outlier).
- Volumes and KD are Semrush estimates. Re-check each keyword in Semrush/Keyword Planner for your target country before writing.

**Questions:** ★ = the question itself appears in the competitor data (volume/KD shown).
The others are suggested questions to confirm in Google's "People also ask" box, AlsoAsked.com or AnswerThePublic before writing.
